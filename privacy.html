Your information

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page describes the data practices of the Ganaveda site code. Ganaveda currently has no sign-in, account profile, or submission form.

Information saved on your device

Practice progress, such as answers attempted, correct answers, session summaries, daily activity, and your best score, is saved in your browser’s local storage on this device. The app uses this information to show your progress and streaks. It is not sent by the app code to a Ganaveda account or backend.

You can remove this data by clearing the site’s stored data in your browser settings. Clearing browser data or changing browsers or devices may also remove or make this progress unavailable.

Answers and voice practice

Typed and selected answers are used in the browser to check practice questions and update your local progress. The app does not save the text you type as a separate response history.

If you choose “Speak answer,” Ganaveda uses the speech-recognition feature supplied by your browser or device. That provider may process audio or a transcript under its own privacy terms. The Ganaveda app reads the returned answer to check the question; it does not save audio or a transcript in its local progress record.

Cookies, analytics, and hosting

The Ganaveda page code does not set analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not include an analytics tracker. Your web host, network, or browser may process technical information needed to deliver or secure the site, such as connection logs or an IP address. Those practices depend on the host and are described by the relevant provider.

External websites

Ganaveda may link to exam authority or other third-party websites. When you follow a link, that website’s privacy policy and practices apply.

Updates

This policy may be revised when the site’s features or data practices change. The date above shows the latest revision. Please review this page from time to time.

This policy reflects the current static site behavior. If the site later adds accounts, analytics, forms, or server-side progress storage, this page should be updated to describe them.
