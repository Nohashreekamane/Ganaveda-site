About Ganaveda

Small practice.
Stronger number sense.

A simple study space for everyday mental math.

What we do

Ganaveda is a browser-based learning tool for building confidence with mental arithmetic and quantitative aptitude. It brings short practice rounds, a daily challenge, and quick references for squares, cubes, and multiplication tables into one place.

Made for steady progress

Choose a topic and pace that suit you, practice a few questions, then use the progress view to notice your effort over time. Exam practice includes original questions generated in your browser for broad topic practice.

Independent study resource

Ganaveda is an independent practice resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by IBPS, the Railway Recruitment Boards, the Staff Selection Commission, or any other examination authority. Its practice tracks are not official exam papers or full mock tests.

Your progress is saved in your browser on your device, so you can practice without creating an account.
